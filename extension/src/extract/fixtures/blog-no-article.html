A practical field guide to tiny gardens

Tiny gardens thrive when the gardener starts with a close look at the available light. A windowsill with morning sun is not the same as a balcony that receives afternoon sun. Choose plants that match the light rather than trying to change the light for the plants.

A container with drainage and a modest amount of compost makes the first growing season easier. Water gently, check the soil every few days, and watch which leaves respond to the changing weather. Keep a record of what worked so the next season can build on those observations.
