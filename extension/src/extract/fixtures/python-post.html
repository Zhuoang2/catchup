Garden tools 2.0 is now available

Sam Example/November 10, 2026
releases

The second release of the garden tools is ready to try. It adds a watering calendar, a seed list, and a soil log.

Download it from the release page, and tell us what works for your garden.

This is an early release, so please do not use it for a large garden yet. The final release is planned for spring.

What is new in garden tools 2.0?

The watering calendar reads rain totals from the weather service, compares them with the needs of each plant, and reminds you only when the soil is likely to be dry.

The seed list keeps the sowing dates, the expected harvest, and the notes you add, so that the next season can start from what worked, what failed, and what you want to try.

The soil log records moisture, temperature, and the time of each reading, and shows a simple chart for each pot, bed, or windowsill box in your garden.

Some features may still change before the final release, and some may be removed if testers find them confusing, slow, or not useful for small gardens.

Thanks to everyone who tested the first release and sent notes about their gardens.
